Immersive title bar

The window's own title bar goes away. The app header becomes the title bar: icon, name, scanner status, Settings, then minimize, maximize and close in the Windows 11 style. Close turns red on hover. Dragging the header moves the window, and double-clicking it maximizes. Snap layouts appear on the maximize button.

Tray icon with status

A 16-pixel glyph made for the notification area, not a shrunken app icon. Its outline follows the taskbar's light or dark colour. A small dot shows scanning (amber) or offline (red). Right-click it for the scan buttons. A notification appears when a file is saved.

One concept everywhere

The chosen concept becomes the .ico (16 to 256 px), the Store and Start logos, the title bar icon and the tray glyph. The scan button icons inside the app stay as they are.
